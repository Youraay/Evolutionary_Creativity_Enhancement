Numerical definitions used for the supplied Gemma-in-loop figure

Gemma fitness: mean saved image rating within a population of 100 images.
Each evolutionary condition uses its own image-only adjective/question and 1–5 scale.
The question templates are supplied separately; no common creativity rerating is implied.

BLIP2 encoder: Salesforce/blip2-opt-2.7b
Revision: 59a1ef6c1e5117b3f65523d1c6066825bcf315e3
Feature: vision_model.pooler_output, 1408 dimensions.
Historical embedding inference used FP16 model weights and RGB 224-pixel inputs.
Distance calculations use float64 raw features and retain population multiplicity.

For one run, let v_i(g) be raw image features in generation g and
c_0 = mean_i v_i(0), the frozen raw generation-0 centroid.

Divergence(g) = mean_i 0.5 * [1 - cosine(v_i(g), c_0)].
Generation 0 is not required to have divergence zero: individual images differ
from the centroid. The reference is run-specific and is never recomputed at later generations.

Diversity(g) = mean_(i != j) 0.5 * [1 - cosine(v_i(g), v_j(g))].
Diagonal self-pairs are excluded. Repeated elites are retained as image observations.
This is the distinct-pair half-cosine convention of the executable evaluator.
The printed manuscript equation has a different half-factor/pair-summation notation;
no identity with its literal printed formula is claimed.

Uncertainty: sample SD (ddof=1) across seed-run metric means, not image-level SD,
SEM or confidence intervals. For target-pooled comparisons, average the three
prompts equally within seed before calculating mean/SD across seeds.

Actual-generation rule: creativity/seed2025/cat and boredom end at generation29.
Their generation29 values and examples remain29. Exclude incomplete seed2025
generation30 prompt-pooled block; do not use chair alone and do not relabel29.

Spearman correlations: rank correlations of VLM scores and the saved per-image
human aggregate scores. Four invalid LLaVA unusualness scores are omitted;
all other cells use36images and that cell32. No participant-level analysis is performed.
