Gemma ratings: generation 30 > generation 0

Primary: six one-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, Holm-adjusted across all six.
Pairing unit: source prompt, using the same valid-image prompt means as the plot.
The same 114 source prompts are included in all six rating dimensions.
Each generation mean covers three runs x 60 images, except omitted invalid scores.
Images and seed runs are not treated as independent replicates for these tests.
Asymptotic normal p-values with tie correction; zero differences discarded (wilcox).
No continuity correction. Differences rounded to 12 decimals to remove floating-point artifacts.
H1 is higher for every rating, including Boredom. No change of direction after seeing results.
n.s. means no significant increase, not evidence of equivalence or no decrease.
This is exploratory/post-hoc, not a preregistered confirmatory analysis.
Signed-rank location interpretation assumes a reasonably symmetric difference distribution.
Inference treats source prompts as independent units; closely related prompts may limit this.
Results concern this prompt set and Gemma ratings, not human perception or all possible prompts.

Sensitivity: complete-endpoint prompts only (excludes one Suitability prompt); same six-test Holm family.
An exact one-sided sign test (with separate six-test Holm correction) is also supplied as a symmetry-robust check.
These sensitivity checks are not replacements selected to obtain significance.
